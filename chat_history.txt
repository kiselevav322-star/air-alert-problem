### Summary of the Pipeline

1. **Dataset Ingestion (`load_dataset`)**:
   - Loads [`official_data_en.csv`](file:///c:/Users/Вікторія/Desktop/air_raid_project/official_data_en.csv) (299,275 rows).
   - Validates columns (`oblast`, `raion`, `hromada`, `level`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `source`).

2. **Timestamp Cleaning (`clean_timestamps`)**:
   - Parses `started_at` and `finished_at` with UTC handling and converts timestamps to local Ukrainian time (`Europe/Kyiv`).
   - Filters missing activation timestamps and calculates alert durations.

3. **Hourly Aggregation (`aggregate_alerts_by_hour`)**:
   - Groups alert start events into 1-hour intervals (`hour_bucket`).
   - Reindexes over a complete chronological date range (`freq='h'`) across the entire 39,279-hour timeline, filling quiet periods with `0`.

4. **Feature Engineering (`create_lag_features`)**:
   - **Autoregressive Lags**: `t-1h`, `t-2h`, `t-3h`, `t-4h`, `t-6h`, `t-12h`, `t-24h` (daily cycle), `t-48h`, and `t-168h` (weekly cycle).
   - **Rolling Window Statistics**: 3h, 6h, 12h, and 24h rolling mean, std, and max (shifted by 1 to strictly prevent data leakage).
   - **Temporal & Cyclical Features**: `hour`, `day_of_week`, `is_weekend`, plus sine/cosine encodings (`sin_hour`, `cos_hour`, `sin_dow`, `cos_dow`).

5. **Baseline Model Training & Evaluation (`train_and_evaluate`)**:
   - Uses a **chronological train/test split** (last 20% reserved for testing) to avoid lookahead bias.
   - Evaluates persistence baselines against scikit-learn models:
     - **Naive Persistence** (`Lag 1h`) & **Seasonal Persistence** (`Lag 24h`)
     - **Ridge Regression**
     - **HistGradientBoostingRegressor**
     - **RandomForestRegressor**
   - Computes **MAE**, **RMSE**, and **$R^2$ Score**, ranks top feature importances, and prints sample predictions.

2. **Spatial-Temporal Cascade Features (`create_spatial_temporal_lag_features`)**:
   - **Spatial Propagation Lags (`spatial_lag_1h`, `spatial_lag_2h`, `spatial_lag_3h`)**:
     For each hour $t$ and oblast $i$, calculates the mean alert intensity across all other $(N - 1 = 24)$ oblasts:
     $$\text{other\_mean}(i, t) = \frac{\sum_{j \neq i} \text{alert\_count}(j, t)}{N - 1}$$

   - **Local Autoregressive Lags**: $t-1\text{h}, t-2\text{h}, t-3\text{h}, t-6\text{h}, t-12\text{h}, t-24\text{h}$.
